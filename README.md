## About

I'm a full-stack engineer from India. I like the whole slice of a product — designing the API, modelling the data, and then building the interface that sits on top of it. Most of my work lives in **TypeScript and JavaScript**, across React/Next.js on the front and Node.js on the back, with a long tail of PHP/Laravel and Angular from earlier years.

Right now I'm spending my learning time on **Go**, distributed systems, and cloud architecture.

- Building full-stack products with React / Next.js and Node.js
- Exploring Go, system design, and how things behave at scale
- Open to **collaboration, freelance, and mentorship** — [drop me a line](mailto:biswassrijeet99@gmail.com)
